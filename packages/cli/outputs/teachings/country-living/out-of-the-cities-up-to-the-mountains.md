# Out of the Cities, Up to the Mountains

**From Seth to the remnant, God's people leave the cities of the plain for a small home on the land, and at the end for the strongholds of the mountains.**

## 1. Eden and the Mountain of God — Gen 2:10; Eze 28:13-14

> God set man's first home on the high ground. The river ran down out of Eden and parted from there.

- _Gen 2:8._ "And the LORD God planted a garden eastward in Eden; and there he put the man whom he had formed." — the first home is a garden, not a city
- _Gen 2:10._ "And a river went out of Eden to water the garden; and from thence it was parted, and became into four heads." — water runs downhill, so Eden is the high ground the four rivers fall from
- _Eze 28:13._ "Thou hast been in Eden the garden of God" — Eden named with
- _Eze 28:14._ "thou wast upon the holy mountain of God" — the mountain of God
- chain: Ex 15:17 (planted in the mountain of inheritance) → Ps 125:1-2 (mountains round about Jerusalem) → Isa 2:2-3 (mountain of the LORD's house in the last days)

## 2. Seth Up to the Mountains, Cain Down to the Cities — Gen 4:16-17

> Cain left God's presence and built the first city. Seth's line went up into the mountains to stay separate, and fell when they came down to mingle.

- _Gen 4:16._ "And Cain went out from the presence of the LORD, and dwelt in the land of Nod, on the east of Eden." — out from God, east, away from the garden
- _Gen 4:17._ "and he builded a city, and called the name of the city, after the name of his son, Enoch." — the first city is Cain's
- _Gen 11:2._ "they found a plain in the land of Shinar; and they dwelt there." — after the Flood, the plain again; Babel follows

[SOP PP 81.1] "He had gone out from the presence of the Lord, cast away the promise of the restored Eden" — Cain's choice

[SOP PP 81.1] "he now founded a city, calling it after the name of his eldest son." — city-building begins with Cain

[SOP PP 81.2] "the latter [Seth's children], in order to escape from their contaminating influence, withdrew to the mountains, and there made their home." — Seth's line goes up

[SOP PP 81.2] "So long as this separation continued, they maintained the worship of God in its purity." — the height kept the worship pure

[SOP PP 81.2] "in the lapse of time they ventured, little by little, to mingle with the inhabitants of the valleys." — the fall came by going down

[SOP CTr 39.2] "The descendants of Seth went up into the mountains and there made themselves homes separate from the sons of Cain." — the same account, a second time

[→] Seth up, Cain down → Abram and Lot make the same split (§3)

## 3. Abram on the Mountain, Lot Down to the Plain — Gen 13:10-12

> Abram built his altar on a mountain. From that height Lot lifted up his eyes, chose the plain, and moved toward Sodom.

- _Gen 12:8._ "And he removed from thence unto a mountain on the east of Bethel, and pitched his tent, [having] Bethel on the west, and Hai on the east: and there he builded an altar unto the LORD, and called upon the name of the LORD." — tent and altar on a mountain
- _Gen 13:3._ "unto the place where his tent had been at the beginning, between Bethel and Hai;" — they are back on that mountain when the strife comes
- _Gen 13:10._ "And Lot lifted up his eyes, and beheld all the plain of Jordan, that it [was] well watered every where" — he looks down from the height
- _Gen 13:11._ "Then Lot chose him all the plain of Jordan; and Lot journeyed east" — east, as Cain went east
- _Gen 13:12._ "Abram dwelled in the land of Canaan, and Lot dwelled in the cities of the plain, and pitched [his] tent toward Sodom." — the plain leads to the cities

[SOP PP 133.1] "Dazzled with visions of worldly gain, Lot overlooked the moral and spiritual evils that would be encountered there." — why the plain drew him

[SOP PP 133.2] "In the free air of those upland plains, with their olive groves and vineyards, their fields of waving grain, and the wide pasture grounds of the encircling hills, he dwelt" — Abram at Hebron, on the uplands

[SOP PP 133.2] "leaving to Lot the perilous luxury of the vale of Sodom." — the vale is the danger

**The way out is up — Gen 19:17:**

- _Gen 14:10._ "and they that remained fled to the mountain." — even in war, the mountain is the refuge
- _Gen 19:17._ "Escape for thy life; look not behind thee, neither stay thou in all the plain; escape to the mountain, lest thou be consumed." — the angel's command
- _Gen 19:30._ "And Lot went up out of Zoar, and dwelt in the mountain" — he ends where Abram began

[SOP CL 7.1] "Lot trod the plain with unwilling and tardy steps." — slow to leave

[SOP CL 30.5] "All that Lot and his family did in Sodom could have been done by them, even if they had lived in a place some distance away from the city." — his work did not need the city home

[SOP CL 30.4] "As did Enoch, we must work in the cities but not dwell in them." — the rule for us

## 4. Elijah and Christ on the Mountain — 1 Ki 19:8; Matt 14:23

> The prophet and the Saviour met God on the mountain. They went down when God sent them.

- _1 Ki 18:42._ "And Elijah went up to the top of Carmel; and he cast himself down upon the earth, and put his face between his knees," — prayer on the height
- _1 Ki 19:8._ "and went in the strength of that meat forty days and forty nights unto Horeb the mount of God." — he flees to the mount of God
- _1 Ki 19:9._ "And he came thither unto a cave, and lodged there; and, behold, the word of the LORD [came] to him" — the word comes on the mountain
- _2 Ki 1:9._ "he sat on the top of an hill. And he spake unto him, Thou man of God, the king hath said, Come down." — the king orders him down
- _2 Ki 1:15._ "Go down with him: be not afraid of him. And he arose, and went down with him unto the king." — he goes down only at God's word

[SOP PK 166.1] "in answer to his prayer, fire flashed from heaven and illuminated the mountaintop." — Carmel

- _Matt 14:23._ "he went up into a mountain apart to pray: and when the evening was come, he was there alone." — Christ alone on the mountain
- _Luke 6:12._ "he went out into a mountain to pray, and continued all night in prayer to God." — a whole night

[SOP CL 14.6] "What were the conditions chosen by the infinite Father for His Son? A secluded home in the Galilean hills" — Christ grew up in the hills

[SOP CL 14.4] "Do not consider it a great deprivation, that you must go into the hills and mountains, but seek for that retirement where you can be alone with God" — the purpose of the hills

## 8. The Strongholds of the Mountains in the Time of Trouble — Matt 24:16

- _Matt 24:16._ "Then let them which be in Judaea flee into the mountains:" — Christ's sign for the flight
- _Luke 21:21._ "Then let them which are in Judaea flee to the mountains; and let them which are in the midst of it depart out; and let not them that are in the countries enter thereinto." — out of the city, and do not go back in
- _Isa 33:15-16._ "He shall dwell on high: his place of defence [shall be] the munitions of rocks: bread shall be given him; his waters [shall be] sure." — the promise for the time of trouble
