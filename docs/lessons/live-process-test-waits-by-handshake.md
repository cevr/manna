### live-process-test-waits-by-handshake

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Kind:** review
- **Said:** "use child-readiness and exit handshakes (watchdog for lost children), and hold the wrapper's cleanup logic deterministically at the spawner seam rather than polling a pid file on a loaded host"
- **Source:** hymnapp/batch-vb-133-findings.md o1 (On base, its first item)
- **Wrong:** A land's merge gate failed once on `@manna/exact-kit`'s `ends what its child left in its process group when the child exits or the run is interrupted`. At load 61 the test ran past bun's 5 s timeout and failed, then passed on the rerun. It polled for a pid file its real child wrote and asked `ps` whether that pid was still alive, so its pass depended on real processes being scheduled within a short wall-time bound. Three more of the package's unit tests also waited on a real child with bun's 5 s as their only bound: exact-cli's iOS run, the probe's first load of effect in a fresh process, and the port claims made from another process. The claim naming the holder of a taken port also gave the holder only 2 s to answer with its card.
- **Cause:** The tests that start real processes waited on them under bun's default timeout, and a loaded host schedules a child late. The wrapper's cleanup rule is now held at the spawner seam, by a stand-in spawner on the test clock. It is a property over the orders of child exit, interrupt and the verify's two-minute stop, and over the children left. Each test that still starts a real process waits only by handshake: the child signals readiness, then its answer, its output's end, its exit or its connection's close is awaited. Each wait runs under `src/watchdog/testing.ts`'s 30 s watchdog, which names what was lost, inside a 45 s test timeout. The port claim takes the holder's answer time as `askMs` (still 2 s by default), and the test gives it the watchdog's time.
- **Guard:** code: `packages/exact-kit/src/run.test.ts` `ends the child's process group, with every child it left there, after the run sees the child exit, when the run is interrupted first, or when a verify is stopped two minutes after its report; never while the child runs on otherwise`; code: `packages/exact-kit/src/run.test.ts` `ends what its child left in its process group when the child exits or the run is interrupted`; code: `packages/exact-kit/src/exact-cli.test.ts` `on an iOS run clears the app simulator's stale launch consoles, says so, and only then runs Exact's CLI`; code: `packages/exact-kit/src/effect/probe.test.ts` `effect, effect/persistence and the runner load for the first time under the data-host rules`; code: `packages/exact-kit/src/drive/port.test.ts` `a claim in another process, which inherits none of this one's, takes another port; its end frees its own`; code: `packages/exact-kit/src/drive/port.test.ts` `waits for a range all taken, naming the holder, and rejects when the wait is out`; code: `packages/exact-kit/src/drive/port.test.ts` `a block claim passes over a block one of whose ports another run holds`
