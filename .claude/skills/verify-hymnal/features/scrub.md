# Held seek

While a hymn plays, a reader holds its words still for 350 ms, drags through its sung lines (48 pt each, down or up) and words (24 pt each, across), sees the chosen line centred with the chosen word underlined, and lifts to play from there. The page draws no button or panel for it (owner, 2026-11-11): the hold and its drag are the whole of it, and the way without a drag is unseen, a screen reader's actions on each sung line (`Seek by line or word`, Previous/Next line and word, `Play from here`, `Cancel`) and, on the web, Enter, the arrows and Escape on the focused words. A development-only synthetic score (the probe) exposes the player's numeric hold state through buttons; it is not a reader gesture or an acoustic word-landing check, and production has no probe content.

## Sub-features

- `scrub-start`: Playing → Held; position freezes and preview makes no commits.
- `scrub-release`: selected sung onset commits once, then playback progresses; duplicate Release does nothing.
- `scrub-cancel`: cancel resumes the frozen origin without a seek; explicit interruption prevents a late restart.
- `scrub-inactive`: Paused and count-in reject Begin without acquisition or movement.
- `scrub-stanzas`: retained original identities map into cut time; changing choice revokes an old hold.
- `scrub-repeat`: repeated occurrences stay distinct; shared onsets keep distinct ordinals; line-only horizontal selection is a no-op.
- `scrub-lines`: the real reader's hold, dragged up or down, chooses later or earlier sung lines; lifting plays from the choice. A quick drag scrolls and never seeks.
- `scrub-words`: a drag across chooses words on the line, diagonally with lines in one drag; line-only lines refuse them; Cancel keeps the frozen origin (tests).
- `scrub-scroll`: with no hold, the page's hand scroll and Resume follow work as before while playing.
- `scrub-pager`: the pager and the back pan still work while playing; an offscreen or covered page never holds.
- `scrub-accessible`: no seek button, panel or status is drawn; the lines' accessibility actions alone begin, step, play and cancel, offered only while they would act, and a one-point clear status carries the choice's numbers while the page holds.
- `scrub-motion`: the same selection and centre at the largest text and both themes; the held scroll is immediate under Reduce Motion.
- `scrub-web`: the web's pointer, touch and keyboard paths, proved by focused handler tests, and live only once the web client is hydrated.

## How to get to it (user POV)

The held seek: open a hymn (`hymnal://hymns/100` / `/hymns/100`), Play, then hold the words still and drag. Nothing on the page names it; a screen reader finds `Seek by line or word` among a sung line's actions. A hold does not take, and the action is not offered, when nothing plays on the page, while paused, during a count-in, and while the player's own place is still pending (a seek not yet answered): the player decides (`hold.canBegin`).

The probe: development deep link only, `hymnal://probe/scrub` / `/probe/scrub`. Fixture settings are isolated and discarded when the route ends.

## Driving it with sim

- **Preconditions:** SKILL.md's doctor passes on this head, own state/port and verify-hymnal simulator. Establish a mounted existing native reader on base before recording base TargetMissing. Read and disable Sound and Sounds and haptics immediately before any Play; the fixture is independently silent.
- **States:** default, open, loading (Acquiring/Landing), empty (production), inactive and count-in; focus-visible and error n/a to this by-hand drive (device failures belong to executable histories).
- **Regression:** `apps/hymnal/src/follow/seek-targets.test.ts`, `apps/hymnal/src/follow/seek-targets.property.slow.test.ts`, `apps/hymnal/src/player/word-seek.test.ts`, `apps/hymnal/src/player/word-seek.property.slow.test.tsx`, `apps/hymnal/src/reader/word-seek.test.ts`, `apps/hymnal/src/reader/WordSeek.test.tsx`, `apps/hymnal/src/reader/WordSeek.web.test.tsx`, `apps/hymnal/src/reader/word-seek.property.slow.test.tsx`.

No journey: a by-hand numeric recipe, with no video, screenshots or retained raw trees. `sim` means `bun run --cwd apps/hymnal sim`, with this run's `--state` on every call. Require each command's JSON `ok`. Read status after every action; retain numeric fields and boolean comparisons only. Read `agent-device snapshot --json --raw --observe-only --force-full --session verify-hymnal` in the drive's own HOME, filtering in memory to the node labelled `player=…` (`scrub-status`). Record player/hold tags, line, word, targetMs, originMs, positionMs, commits, commitMs and choice. Negative word means no word, not ordinal zero. Two reads are separate host-clock observations; no timing claim when doctor says timingsHold=false.

**Silent setup/restore.** `sim open settings --expect "label=Sounds and haptics"`; read its switch value in memory, save the boolean, and `sim tap "label=Sounds and haptics"` only if on. Leave/reopen settings and confirm 0. `sim open hymns/100 --expect "label=Sound"`; read Sound's checked/unchecked value in memory, save it and `sim tap "label=Sound"` only if checked. Reopen and confirm unchecked. Immediately before each fixture Play confirm both remain off; make no other global setting changes. Finally restore both through these controls, leave/reopen each view and confirm the original booleans. Keep booleans only.

1. **Start (`scrub-start`).** `sim open probe/scrub --expect id=scrub-begin`; `sim tap id=scrub-play` → Playing. `sim tap id=scrub-begin` → Held/Paused, finite originMs=positionMs. Read twice at least one second apart → position unchanged. Save commits=C. `sim tap id=scrub-next-line`; `sim tap id=scrub-next-word` → preview changes, commits=C, position still origin. Repeated selection at a boundary is a no-op.
2. **Release (`scrub-release`).** Save preview target T and C; `sim tap id=scrub-release` → Free/Playing, commits=C+1, commitMs=T. A later read has positionMs>T. `sim tap id=scrub-release` → commits unchanged and playback continues. Fixture line starts are 1000, 6000, 11000 and 16000; word onsets are start+100/start+200 except the shared pair on line 2.
3. **Cancel/external intent (`scrub-cancel`).** `sim tap id=scrub-play`; `sim tap id=scrub-begin`; save origin O and C. `sim tap id=scrub-next-line`; `sim tap id=scrub-cancel` → Playing/Free, commits=C, position progresses from O rather than preview. Repeat Begin, then `sim tap id=scrub-pause`, Release, Cancel → Paused/Free, no restart/commit. Repeat Play, Begin, `sim tap id=scrub-invalidate`, Release, Cancel → no restart/commit. Repeat Play, Begin, `sim tap id=scrub-replace`, Release → replacement owns the player, no old target commit. Route exit and inactive/background revoke; foreground alone never restores an old hold.
4. **Inactive (`scrub-inactive`).** Play, `sim tap id=scrub-pause`; save C/position, `sim tap id=scrub-begin` → Free/Paused, unchanged. `sim tap id=scrub-count-in`, then Begin while numeric countIn>=0 → Free, commits unchanged. countIn=-1 means not counting, even if the player tag says Playing; repeat a trial that missed the count. The synthetic tempo leaves time to read it. Do not claim other inactive tags from this drive.
5. **Stanzas (`scrub-stanzas`).** Play, Begin, `sim tap id=scrub-stanza` → old hold Free, late Release makes no commit. Choice is now 2. Play, Begin → original line 2, shared word onset 1100 (cut from 11100); next line is original line 3 at 6000, never line 0 or 1. Release lands at that cut target. `sim tap id=scrub-stanza` restores all. Leaving/reopening discards fixture choices.
6. **Repeated/shared/line-only (`scrub-repeat`).** All stanzas: Play, Begin; Previous line until line 0, then Next line twice → line 2, not line 0 despite identical synthetic text. Next word selects word 0 at11100, Next word selects word 1 also at11100: distinct ordinal, equal time, no commits. Release → commitMs11100. Play, Begin, step to line 3 → Line at16000, word=-1; Previous/Next word leave preview unchanged. Release → commitMs16000. This does not promise a uniquely lit shared word. Corpus/663 accuracy is the slow sweep.

**The held reader (rows 7–13).** On head, after the silent setup above, read in memory only: no node anywhere with the id `hymn-word-seek`, `reader-seek-dock` or `reader-seek-controls`, and no button named `Seek by line or word`, `Previous line`, `Next line`, `Previous word`, `Next word`, `Play from here` or `Cancel` (the bar's own `Seek` slider, `id=seek`, is the player's); `Position`'s value from the bar (`id=seek`) or `Open player`; the shown page's `line` nodes (inside the `hymn-scroll` within the screen's frame) and their rects; `id=hymn-number`. The driver reads nothing while a gesture runs (agent-device runs one command at a time; a snapshot sent during a hold returns after it), so a drag is read by what its lift did: a release seeks `Position` to the chosen line, a quick drag only scrolls. A hold's marker, status and centring are proved by `WordSeek.test.tsx` and the acceptance file, not live.

A hold-then-drag is agent-device's own `gesture drag <from> <to> <holdMs> <moveMs> <endHoldMs>`, run with the state's HOME (`HOME=<state>/home agent-device gesture drag @<ref> @<ref> 900 800 300 --session verify-hymnal`), from a `line` node's ref to another node's: the finger stays still `holdMs` on the first, moves to the second over `moveMs`, waits `endHoldMs` and lifts. `sim` has no such command. Translation is the second node's centre less the first's: up is later lines, 48 pt a line; right is later words, 24 pt a word.

7. **Lines (`scrub-lines`).** `sim open hymns/100 --expect "label=Sound"`; `sim tap "label=Play hymn 100"`; after the count-in read `Position` P0. Drag from the 10th shown `line` to the 2nd (dy about −330, hold 900, move 800, end 300), read `Position` P1 at once → P1 is P0 plus far more than the wall time between the reads (r7: 1:53 → 2:48 in 7 s, six lines later), still `Pause hymn 100`, still hymn 100. Native continuous drag on a real finger stays owner-hand proof.
8. **Words and diagonal (`scrub-words`).** Playing, read P0; drag from the 2nd shown `line` to `Pause hymn 100`'s button (dx about +167, dy about +625: earlier lines and later words at once) → `Position` falls back (r7: 3:08 → 1:31), hymn 100 still shown (the pager never turned), still playing. Which word the lift chose is not readable live: word steps, the whole-word underline, line-only and shared cases are `WordSeek.test.tsx` and the acceptance file (`apps/hymnal/src/test/reader-word-seek.tsx`, fixture evidence, named apart).
9. **Hand scroll (`scrub-scroll`).** With no hold and Play running, a quick drag (`gesture drag` with hold 30, end 0) between the same two lines → `Position` advances only by the wall time (no seek) and the lines' rects move (the page scrolled). Then `sim scroll screen down`, then `sim scroll screen up` (`sim scroll id=hymn-scroll` takes an offscreen neighbour's scroll: follow.md, Gotchas) → `Resume follow` shown, player still Playing, no `reader-seek-marker`, `Position` advancing. `sim tap "label=Resume follow"` → the selected line about a third of the way down `hymn-scroll` again. `sim hold` alone proves a still acquire and release only, never a drag.
10. **Pager (`scrub-pager`).** Playing 100: `sim scroll screen right` → hymn 101 shown (a link's pages run in number order), `Pause hymn 100` still shown, `Position` advancing; a hold-then-drag on 101's words (row 7's drag) leaves `Position` advancing by the wall time alone (its hymn does not play, so no hold takes); `sim scroll screen left` → 100 again. `sim gesture edge-pan` → back to the list, audio unchanged. Row 8's diagonal drag leaves hymn 100 shown: a held horizontal drag never pages.
11. **No control drawn (`scrub-accessible`).** Idle, Playing and after each drag of rows 7–9, the read finds none of the seek's ids or button names (above). The actions themselves cannot be performed by the driver (XCUITest has no custom-action call): `WordSeek.test.tsx` performs them as the platform does (each line's `onAccessibilityAction` with the action's name) and proves Begin → line step → word step → Play from here, Begin → Cancel, and the actions offered only while they act (none while Paused, during a count-in or a pending place); `WordSeek.web.test.tsx` proves Escape, and the guard that no seek button or panel is drawn and the status stays one clear point. VoiceOver itself (the rotor, speech, the announcement) stays owner-hand proof.

Reduce Motion on and off only by the orchestrator's simulator-only method: the held scroll is immediate both ways (the handler's animated flag false under Reduce Motion), no pulse. Restore the text size and appearance with a second view. Feel stays owner review.
13. **Web (`scrub-web`).** The web handlers are proved by `WordSeek.web.test.tsx` and the slow acceptance file (primary pointer only, lost capture cancels once, diagonal and reversal, Escape and the keyboard path). The Sideshow page is a standalone audition, not the One reader. ## Gotchas

- A current refused status observation closes an owned hold, cancels its pending work and leaves the player Failed; late Select/Release/Cancel cannot restart it. Older cancelled observations cannot close a newer hold. Executable status-completion histories cover failures, delayed answers and replacement races in every owned phase on both Sound paths; this live recipe does not inject device failures.

- On base or production `sim open probe/scrub --expect id=scrub-begin` is TargetMissing; a broken base reader is infrastructure-blocked, not feature-red.
- Controls may require `sim scroll screen down` or `sim scroll screen up`; read status independently of control visibility. Restart Play per trace so the short score has not ended.
- The counter observes seeks across the normal score-clock boundary: compare deltas per trace. Refused device retries remain one logical player act (covered by controlled tests).
- The probe enables no One Audio, microphone, real recording, haptics or continuous hold-and-drag.
- `sim` has a hold and a pan, never one continuous hold-then-drag; agent-device's `gesture drag` (rows 7–10) is one, synthesized by XCUITest on the simulator. It proves the recognizer takes a still hold and follows the drag that comes after it, with the page's scroll and the pager, on the simulator. It does not prove a real finger's feel, multitouch, acoustic word landing, actual haptics or VoiceOver navigation: those are the owner's on a Release build from main, never a Debug or prototype build.
- The held reader's receipts are numeric and boolean only: `Position`, rects, selected booleans, the absence of seek controls, page number. No raw tree, console, screenshot, video or hymn text.
- No seek control is drawn: a node named `Seek` with `id=seek` is the bar's position slider, not the held seek. A hold that took and was lifted with no drag still seeks once, to the sounding place, so `Position` may step back by part of a word.
- Open the reader by link (`sim open hymns/100`). A hymn opened while a sheet is up (Settings, the player, About, a chord) closes the sheet and is pushed where it was (`settings-hymn-over`), so the bar and its `#` stay over the page as on any other route.
