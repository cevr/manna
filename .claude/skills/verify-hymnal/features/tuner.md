# Tuner

The app listens to the microphone to tune a guitar. On iOS a Swift module
(`Listen`) records nothing and plays nothing: it takes the input in
2048-sample frames and hands each to the pitch reader, and the system asks
the reader for the microphone with the text `Hymnal listens to your guitar
to tune it. Nothing is recorded or kept.` Android and the web have no
tuner.

## Sub-features

- `tuner-native-build`: `sim install --prebuild` of the branch builds and launches the app, and the built `Info.plist` has `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` with the usage text.
- `tuner-open-chord`: a hymn → a chord → the chord sheet's `Tuner` → the tuner full screen (`id=tuner-screen`, no grabber) over it; `Done` returns to the chord sheet at its own detent, and the log holds `[tuner] stop reason=closed` when it listened.
- `tuner-open-settings`: Settings → `Tuner` → the tuner full screen (`id=tuner-screen`).
- `tuner-bar-clear`: on hymn 3, the `Player` region holds `Key`, `Open player`, `Sound` and `Play hymn 3`, and no `label=Tuner`; with hymn 100 sounding over hymn 3, the bar holds no `label=Tuner` either.
- `tuner-open-key`: with the permission revoked and chords on, hymn 3's `Key` → `id=dials` holds `id=dials-tuner` (`label=Tuner`, a button with the hint `Opens the guitar tuner`) above `id=dial-capo` and `id=dial-key`; with chords off, `Key` → `id=dial-key` and `id=dials-tuner` alone. With chords on, `id=dials-tuner` → `id=tuner-screen` (Argent `describe` lists only the tuner's nodes) reading `Microphone is off for Hymnal`, and no `[feel] play` or `[tuner] listen` line.
- `tuner-key-closes`: from `tuner-open-key`, `Done` → hymn 3 (`label=Stanza 1`) with no `id=dials` and no `id=dials-backdrop` in the tree, `Key` not expanded; one tap on `Key` opens the dials again.
- `tuner-chip-size`: with chords on, hymn 3's `Key` held 900 ms opens `id=dials`; its `id=dials-tuner` frame is 32 points tall, the Reset chip's height (Reset shows only after a dial turn, so its size is held by the unit test, not this drive), and the chip's mark is a small upright fork with no label of its own (`aria-hidden`).
- `tuner-bar-room`: with Sound and `Sounds and haptics` off, hymn 100 playing and hymn 3 shown, the opener's width W and the screen's width S give `W − (S − 375) ≥ 100` (the opener is the row's only flexible item), and `Key`, `Play hymn 3`, `Sound` and `Pause hymn 100` are each at least 44 × 44.
- `tuner-pick`: `hymnal://tuner?feed=A2:0`, then `sim tap id=tuner-string-E2` → `id=tuner-reading` is labelled `Low E string, 500 cents sharp` (±2), `id=tuner-hint` reads `Loosen`, `id=tuner-auto` is unchecked, and the log holds `[tuner] target id=E2`.
- `tuner-auto`: from `tuner-pick`, `sim tap id=tuner-auto` → `A string, in tune`, `id=tuner-auto` is checked, and the log holds `[tuner] target id=auto`. With `feed=E2:-12` and Auto on, the reading names `Low E`.
- `tuner-pick-again`: with the permission revoked, Settings → `Tuner`. A tap on `id=tuner-string-D3` chooses D: it is `[selected]` with the hint `Goes back to Auto`, `id=tuner-auto` is unchecked, and the log holds `[tuner] target id=D3`. A second tap on it → no string `[selected]`, no `Goes back to Auto` hint, `id=tuner-auto` checked, and `[tuner] target id=auto`. D, then `id=tuner-string-A2` → only A `[selected]`, with the hint. The switch from Auto → `E2` `[selected]` with the hint (`target id=E2`). A tap on E → Auto again.
- `tuner-covered`: listening on `feed=E2:-12`, open `hymnal://hymns/100` (it opens as a sheet over the tuner) → `[tuner] stop reason=covered`, and no `[tuner] frame` or `[tuner] announced` line after it. The sheet pulled down, back to the tuner → a new `[tuner] listen source=feed`.
- `tuner-announce`: on `feed=E2:-12`, over 10 s the log holds between 1 and 10 `[tuner] announced` lines, and none holds the reading's words. A steady feed gives about 1: it announces only when the words change, at most once a second.
- `tuner-permission`: with the permission reset, opening the tuner runs the system request (`[tuner] permission state=undetermined`, then the alert in the tree with the usage text); Allow → `[tuner] permission state=granted`.
- `tuner-denied`: with the permission revoked, the screen reads `Microphone is off for Hymnal` and holds `Open Settings`, and no `id=tuner-reading` (no `label=Listening`, nothing read after `Open Settings`); no `[tuner] listen` line.
- `tuner-listen`: with the permission granted, `hymnal://tuner?feed=E2:-12` → `id=tuner-reading` is labelled `Low E string, 12 cents flat` (±2), and the log holds `[tuner] listen source=feed`.
- `tuner-tuned`: `feed=A2:0` → `id=tuner-string-A2` is labelled `A string, tuned` within 2 s; the other five strings are not tuned.
- `tuner-pauses-player`: with Sound off, hymn 100 playing, the tuner opened from Settings → the player reads Paused and the log holds `[tuner] paused player` once; after the tuner closes the player stays Paused.
- `tuner-no-sound`: through every row above, no `[feel] play` line comes from the tuner, and Play is pressed only in `tuner-pauses-player` and `tuner-bar-room`, with Sound off.
- `tuner-speed`: over at least 50 `[tuner] frame ms=` lines of the feed, the median is at most 8 ms and the p95 at most 16 ms.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- A hymn page's `Key` (the bar's key button) → the panel's `Tuner` (the top row's leading chip), a hymn's chord sheet → `Tuner` (in its head, beside `Done`), or Settings → `Tuner`; each opens the tuner full screen at `/tuner`, and `Done` (top left) closes it. `Auto` (top right) and the six string circles of the headstock pick the target. Deep link: `hymnal://tuner`; in development `hymnal://tuner?feed=<string>:<cents>` (for example `A2:-12`) feeds frames in place of the microphone.

## Driving it with sim

- **Preconditions:** the baseline's boot; a load of at most 12 and 10 GB free before a cold build (SKILL.md, Launch).
- **States:** default. Focus-visible, open, loading, empty and error are n/a.
- **Regression:** `apps/hymnal/src/tuner/session.property.test.ts` (the listen, permission and player rule over generated worlds), `apps/hymnal/src/tuner/guard.test.ts` (the declaration equals the contract One derives from `Listen.swift`; the module names no output or player; the session is `.record` with `.measurement` and gives the audio back with `.notifyOthersOnDeactivation` in the category, mode and options it found, unless another owner took the session), `apps/hymnal/src/tuner/pitch.test.ts` and `pitch.property.slow.test.ts` (the pitch reader and the reading), `apps/hymnal/src/tuner/autotap.test.tsx` (the screen mounted: the string pick, the second tap back to Auto and the switch, over every target and press).

1. **Build (`tuner-native-build`).** `sim install --prebuild` → `{"ok":true,"command":"install",…}` and the app shows `label=Hymns`.
2. **Usage text.** `/usr/libexec/PlistBuddy -c 'Print :NSMicrophoneUsageDescription' apps/hymnal/ios/build/dd/Build/Products/Debug-iphonesimulator/Hymnal.app/Info.plist` → `Hymnal listens to your guitar to tune it. Nothing is recorded or kept.`
3. **The log.** The `[tuner]` lines are JS console lines, which `sim log` does not see. Read them with Argent: `argent run debugger-connect --port $PORT --device_id <udid>`, then `argent run debugger-log-registry --port $PORT --device_id <udid> --json` names the log file to grep. A reconnect starts a new file. Each later step reads its lines from that file.
4. **Opened with no feed (`tuner-open-settings`, `tuner-denied`).** Run `xcrun simctl privacy <udid> revoke microphone im.cvr.hymnal` first, so a sheet opened with no feed starts nothing. `sim open settings --expect label=Simple`, `sim scroll label=Tuner down`, `sim tap label=Tuner` → `id=tuner-screen`, `label=Microphone is off for Hymnal`, `label=Open Settings`, no `id=tuner-reading` and no `label=Listening`, and no `[tuner] listen` line.
5. **From the chord sheet (`tuner-open-chord`).** With chords on, `sim open hymns/3 --expect "label=Stanza 1"`, `sim tap id=chord-1-1-1` → the chord sheet with `label=Tuner` beside `label=Done`. `sim tap label=Tuner` → `id=tuner-screen`, full screen with no grabber. `sim tap label=Done` → the chord sheet again (`id=chord-box-0`), at its `Half screen` detent.
   **The bar (`tuner-bar-clear`).** `sim open hymns/3 --expect "label=Stanza 1"` → the `Player` region holds `label=Key`, `label=Open player`, `label=Sound` and `label=Play hymn 3`, and no `label=Tuner`. With hymn 100 sounding (as in `tuner-bar-room`) and hymn 3 shown, the bar holds no `label=Tuner` either.
   **From the key panel (`tuner-open-key`, `tuner-key-closes`).** With the permission revoked and chords on, `sim open hymns/3 --expect "label=Stanza 1"`, `sim tap label=Key` → `id=dials` holds `id=dials-tuner` (`label=Tuner`, hint `Opens the guitar tuner`), its frame above `id=dial-capo` and `id=dial-key`. A 450 ms hold of `Key` turns chords off (as `chords-and-capo.md` drives it); `sim tap label=Key` → only `id=dial-key` and `id=dials-tuner`. Turn chords on again. `sim tap id=dials-tuner` → `id=tuner-screen`; Argent `describe` lists only the tuner's nodes, the screen reads `label=Microphone is off for Hymnal`, and the log has no `[feel] play` and no `[tuner] listen` line. `sim tap label=Done` → hymn 3 (`label=Stanza 1`), no `id=dials` and no `id=dials-backdrop`, `label=Key` not expanded; one `sim tap label=Key` opens the dials again.
   **Room in the bar (`tuner-bar-room`).** With `Sound` and `Sounds and haptics` off (as in step 8), on hymn 100 `sim tap "label=Play hymn 100"` → `label=Pause hymn 100`; `sim open hymns/3` → the bar's compact `label=Play hymn 3`. Read the opener's (`label=Open player`) frame width W and the screen's width S: `W − (S − 375) ≥ 100`, since only the opener flexes. `Key`, `Play hymn 3`, `Sound` and `Pause hymn 100` are each at least 44 × 44. Pause, then turn `Sound` and `Sounds and haptics` on again.
6. **The request (`tuner-permission`).** Run `xcrun simctl privacy <udid> reset microphone im.cvr.hymnal`, then `sim open 'tuner?feed=E2:0'` twice (see Gotchas). → `[tuner] permission state=undetermined`, and the system alert with the usage text is in the tree. Tap `Allow` → `[tuner] permission state=granted`, then `[tuner] listen source=feed`.
7. **A reading (`tuner-listen`, `tuner-tuned`).** With the permission granted, `sim open 'tuner?feed=E2:-12' --expect id=tuner-string-E2` → `id=tuner-reading` is labelled `Low E string, 12 cents flat`. `sim open 'tuner?feed=A2:0'` → within 2 s, `id=tuner-string-A2` is labelled `A string, tuned` and no other `tuner-string-*` is tuned.
8. **The player (`tuner-pauses-player`).** Turn the bar's `Sound` off (`value` `unchecked`) and `Sounds and haptics` off in Settings. On hymn 100, `sim tap "label=Play hymn 100"` (with `tuner-bar-room`, one of the two Plays of the drive) → `label=Pause hymn 100`. Open Settings and `sim open 'tuner?feed=E2:0'` → exactly one `[tuner] paused player` line, before the `listen` line. `sim back`, then `sim open hymns/100` → the bar reads `label=Play hymn 100`, and its place does not move for 8 s. Turn `Sound` and `Sounds and haptics` on again.
   **A picked string (`tuner-pick`, `tuner-auto`).** `sim open 'tuner?feed=A2:0'`, then `sim tap id=tuner-string-E2` → `id=tuner-reading` is labelled `Low E string, 500 cents sharp` (±2), `id=tuner-hint` reads `Loosen`, `id=tuner-auto` has `value` unchecked, and the log holds `[tuner] target id=E2`. `sim tap id=tuner-auto` → `A string, in tune`, `id=tuner-auto` checked, and `[tuner] target id=auto`. `sim open 'tuner?feed=E2:-12'` → the reading names `Low E`.
   **Picked again (`tuner-pick-again`).** With no feed: after step 4's revoke, Settings → `Tuner` (`label=Microphone is off for Hymnal`), with `Sounds and haptics` off. Only string and switch taps; no Play. Read each `[tuner] target` line from step 3's log file. `sim tap id=tuner-string-D3` → `sim snapshot` shows `id=tuner-string-D3` `[selected]` with the hint `Goes back to Auto`, `id=tuner-auto` `value` unchecked, and `[tuner] target id=D3`. `sim tap id=tuner-string-D3` again → no `tuner-string-*` `[selected]`, no `Goes back to Auto`, `id=tuner-auto` checked, and `[tuner] target id=auto`. `sim tap id=tuner-string-D3`, then `sim tap id=tuner-string-A2` → only `id=tuner-string-A2` `[selected]`, with the hint, and `target id=A2`. `sim tap id=tuner-string-A2` → Auto. `sim tap id=tuner-auto` → `id=tuner-string-E2` `[selected]` with the hint, and `target id=E2` (nothing heard, so the lowest). `sim tap id=tuner-string-E2` → Auto again, `target id=auto`. `tuner-no-sound` holds: no `[feel] play`, no `[tuner] listen` and no Swift `[tuner]` line.
   **Covered (`tuner-covered`).** Listening on `feed=E2:-12`, `sim open hymns/100` → the log holds `[tuner] stop reason=covered` and no `[tuner] frame` line after it. The hymn opens as a sheet over the full-screen tuner, with no back button, so `sim back` fails (`in-app back control is not available`): pull the sheet down, `sim gesture pan id=hymn-about 0 500 --ms 300` → `id=tuner-screen` again and a new `[tuner] listen source=feed`.
   **Announced (`tuner-announce`).** On `feed=E2:-12` for 10 s, count the `[tuner] announced` lines: between 1 and 10, none with words after `announced`.
9. **Speed (`tuner-speed`).** Leave `feed=E2:-12` open until the log holds at least 50 `[tuner] frame ms=` lines (about 40 s), at a load of at most 12. Take the median and the p95 of the numbers.
10. **No sound (`tuner-no-sound`).** Over the whole drive, the log holds no `[feel] play` line from the tuner, and no Swift `[tuner]` line (the feed never starts `Listen`).

## Gotchas

- Never let the simulator open the Mac's microphone: a drive sets the permission with `xcrun simctl privacy <udid> grant|revoke|reset microphone im.cvr.hymnal` before launch, and feeds the tuner frames instead of the microphone.
- A tuner opened with the permission granted and no feed starts the real microphone. Open the tuner with no feed only with the permission revoked. Every listening row opens the feed link.
- Opened over a sheet (the chord sheet, Settings), the tuner covers the screen, yet `sim snapshot` still lists the nodes under it (`Sheet Grabber`, the hymn's `Stanza` labels). Prove "full screen, no grabber" with Argent's `describe` (the accessibility service's view of the screen): it lists only the tuner's own nodes, with `Done` at the top.
- `xcrun simctl privacy` ends the app. The next `sim open` lands on the hymn list and fails its `--expect`: run it again.
- A development build mounts the screen twice (React's StrictMode): each open logs two `[tuner] permission state=` lines and one `listen` line.
- The system alert makes the app inactive and then active again: after `Allow` the log holds `granted`, `listen`, `stop reason=away`, a second permission read and a second `listen`.
- `tuner-pauses-player` opens the feed link from Settings, not Settings' `Tuner` row: with the permission granted, the row would start the real microphone. The pause runs the same path.
- The build may rewrite `apps/hymnal/native/feel/Voice.d.swift.ts`; put it back with `git checkout -- apps/hymnal/native/feel/Voice.d.swift.ts`.
- Only the owner's phone proves a real string: the microphone on hardware, a plucked string and the hand-off with a playing hymn.
