The people are the young mother at the wedding feast and the baby she holds, never named. She is a young woman of slender build, about three and a third heads tall and nine tenths of a man's height, dressed in the Bible's own time: a long dusty-blue dress (#5f7fa8, its far side turned toward a deeper blue-violet #4c5687) to her ankles with long sleeves to the wrist, the same blue from each shoulder to each round warm-peach hand, never a cream or white sleeve; a cream veil (#eadcc2, its shade turned toward lavender #c8bdd6) over her brown hair, falling to her shoulders; brown sandals. Her skin warm peach (#f2b27c, its shade a rosy violet #c9877e); a face of three marks: two short brows, two small dark oval eyes and a one-line mouth. The baby is about two heads tall, wrapped in a cream wrap (#eadcc2) to its feet, a round warm-peach face with the same three marks and a small tuft of brown hair; its round hands at the ends of the wrap's folds. Standing alone on a turnaround, she stands at rest with both arms hanging in their sleeves; with the baby, she holds it on her hip with exactly two hands, both on the baby: one under it, one at its back, unless a pose gives one hand a cup. Each hand at the end of its sleeve, no thumbs, no fingers. No pattern, trim or embroidery. What they hold, and only where a pose names it: a small plain clay cup.
